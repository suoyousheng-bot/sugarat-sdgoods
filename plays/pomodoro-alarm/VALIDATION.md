# Pomodoro Alarm validation

Recorded results for the community release. They describe the recorded build and devices only; a rebuilt or modified image needs its own verification.

## Community release notes

The retained v1.0.0 ESP32-S3 application image passed header, segment checksum and appended SHA-256 validation. Its SHA-256 matches the image recorded as delivered by Saycraft on 2026-11-17. Source inspection confirms the timer controls and audio reminder implementation. Physical screen, touch, completed-cycle audio, sleep behavior and launcher coexistence have not been independently verified for this publication.
