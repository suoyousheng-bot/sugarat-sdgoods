# Sugarat Social Card validation

Recorded results for the community release. They describe the recorded build and devices only; a rebuilt or modified image needs its own verification.

## Community release notes

Current 0.1.2: ESP32-S3 image compilation, checksum and SHA-256 inspection; native ASan/UBSan storage, crypto/protocol, HTTP and language checks; real LVGL gestures/rendering; mobile browser editing and save/error handling. Hardware evidence is from 0.1.1: standalone flashing and independent Flash verification passed, and the owner confirmed normal screen display after USB reconnect. 0.1.2 hardware startup, physical touch, phone hotspot/QR use, two-device RF transfer and launcher installation/Exit have not been verified. Serial capture and diagnostic software restart remain unverified.
